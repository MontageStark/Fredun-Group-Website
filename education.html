Our Mission

Education as a Foundation for Progress

At Fredun Group, we believe that every individual — regardless of ability or background — deserves the opportunity to learn and grow. We actively support education for the underprivileged, orphaned, and specially-abled communities through dedicated initiatives like Inkling India.

We provide scholarships, school supplies, and infrastructure support to help bridge the education gap. Our volunteers conduct mentoring sessions, skill-building workshops, and career guidance programs. Through partnerships with local schools and NGOs, we extend our reach to remote and underserved areas.

Flagship Initiative

Inkling India – Hope Through Learning

Inkling India is our flagship educational initiative aimed at creating inclusive learning environments for disabled individuals and those without families. Through custom-designed curriculums, sign language training, mobility-accessible classrooms, and digital education tools, we break the barriers that prevent them from learning.

We work closely with special educators and therapists to tailor teaching methods to individual needs. Vocational training programs equip students with practical skills for independent living. Community engagement events foster awareness and acceptance of differently-abled learners.

Inclusive Learning

Education for Differently-Abled Students

We provide special training programs, visual aids, and adaptive learning materials that cater to students with hearing, vision, and physical impairments. Our volunteer educators undergo disability sensitization to create a safe and encouraging environment for all learners.

We implement assistive technologies such as screen readers and audio interfaces to enhance accessibility. Regular workshops build self-esteem and promote social integration among differently-abled students. Collaboration with rehabilitation centers and special schools strengthens our outreach and expertise.

Beyond the Classroom

Vocational Training & Skill Development

Education isn't limited to books. Our vocational centers teach skills like tailoring, computer literacy, spoken English, art & craft, and basic healthcare training. These practical courses help our students gain employment and build confidence.

Certification is provided upon completion, enhancing employability. Special attention is given to women and differently-abled individuals to promote financial independence. Our trainers are industry professionals who mentor students through real-world projects.

Community Care

Empowering Orphans & Homeless Youth

We collaborate with local shelters and foster homes to bring structured learning programs and mentorship to children without families. These initiatives provide not only education but also emotional care and guidance for a brighter future.

We offer life skills training, career counseling, and personality development workshops to prepare them for independent living. Nutritional support and healthcare checkups ensure their overall well-being. Our mentors act as consistent role models, fostering trust and long-term growth.
